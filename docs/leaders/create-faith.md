# How to create a faith

Open `/faith browse` → **Create faith**. Seven screens take you from a name to a finished faith. The boxes at the top show your progress.

You must be Agnostic or unaffiliated. Otherwise, [leave your current faith first](../players/join-faith.md#leave-or-switch-faith).

## 1. Name and values

Enter a unique **Faith name** of 2–40 characters. In **Core values**, describe what the faith believes, then choose **Continue**. This description does not set gameplay rules.

## 2. Choose your symbol

Pick a symbol; a green checkbox marks your choice. Use **Previous**, **Next** or **Search** to browse, then **Continue**. Symbols are artwork and do not apply religious rules.

<!-- Screenshot: symbol gallery with a selected head, filling boxes and the control row. -->

## 3. Your community

Choose **Who can join**:

- **Open:** anyone can join.
- **Application:** clergy approve requests.
- **Invitation:** clergy invite people.

Set the **Clergy title**, interfaith marriage choice and **People per marriage**, then **Continue**. Two means a pair; the server limits larger groups.

## 4. Food rules

Combine **Vegetarian**, **Vegan**, **No pork**, **No beef**, **No alcohol** and **Gluten-free**, or choose individual foods and ingredients through **Customize foods**. Select nothing to leave food unrestricted, then **Continue**.

The screen shows the penalty for known violations. Uncertain ingredients cause no penalty.

## 5. Choose ceremonies

Open **Simple prayer**, **Shared meal**, **Baptism** or another example. Adjust its name and requirements, then choose **Use ceremony**. A checkbox marks each included ceremony.

Choose several examples, or continue with none. You can [edit their steps later](ceremonies.md).

## 6. Holy days

Choose **Add weekly gathering**, **Add full-moon gathering** or another example. Hover to see its next date. Set its name and schedule, then use **Time and activity** for an all-day observance, fast or selected ceremony.

Choose **Use holy day**, then **Review faith**. You can skip this step. Dates use the real-world server timezone shown on screen. See [holy days](../players/holy-days.md) and [calendar setup](people-and-places.md#plan-a-holy-day) for details.

## 7. Review and create

Check the summary and use **Edit** to revisit any section. Select **Create faith** to become its founder. Then choose **Open faith** to try it or **Advanced settings** to keep editing.

<!-- Screenshot: final Review showing chosen food rules, ceremony and holy day. -->

??? tip "Saving and going back"

    Only the final **Create faith** saves the setup. Back keeps your choices; cancelling or disconnecting discards them. Optional sections can stay empty.

    If creation is unavailable, check your current membership and server permissions. Founders with other members must choose a successor before leaving.
