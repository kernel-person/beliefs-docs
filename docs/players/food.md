# Food

Check **/faith → Food** before choosing a meal. Each faith sets its own food rules, even when its name resembles a real-world tradition.

## Read the food guide

1. Open **Food → Foods**.
2. Use **Search** to find a food by name and hover over it to read its status and reason.
3. Open **Diets** to see the faith's selected dietary requirements, or **Rules** for its individual restrictions.

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Permitted** | The available facts satisfy these food rules. |
| **Forbidden** | A known item, ingredient, category, tag or dietary fact conflicts with a rule. |
| **Uncertain** | Information needed to decide is missing. |
| **Depends on ingredients** | This is a variable dish; its actual ingredients matter. |

Beliefs records eating. A confirmed forbidden food also records a food violation and displays the reason in chat; its effect on your score follows your faith's teachings. An uncertain result is not automatically a food violation. Read [Your life](your-life.md) to understand the record.

## Custom dishes

When the server has a compatible **Heirloom** integration, the catalogue can include custom foods and ingredient facts. Two versions of a dish may have different ingredients. A catalogue preview with **Depends on ingredients** is not a promise about every crafted version.

??? tip "A food is missing or its status is unclear"

    Clear the search, check the filter and try the next page. Missing ingredient information stays uncertain. Ask the faith's founder about its rules, or the server owner about custom-food support. Players read food rules here; founders edit them through `/faith settings`.

Fasting is a separate voluntary activity. See [Ceremonies and fasting](ceremonies.md#fasting-and-community-gatherings).
