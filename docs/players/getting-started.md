# Start here

Run `/faith`. This is your everyday menu for beliefs, worship and family. New players start in **Agnostic** and can stay there while exploring.

## Pick your first activity

- [Join a faith](join-faith.md), or [create your own](../leaders/create-faith.md).
- [Pray](prayer.md) and [join a ceremony](ceremonies.md).
- [Check what you can eat](food.md) or [see upcoming holy days](holy-days.md).
- [Marry](marriage.md), [adopt](adoption.md), or [open your family tree](family-tree.md).

The optional **First steps** guide is available with `/faith guide`. Founders use `/faith settings` to [modify their faith](../leaders/modify-faith.md); `/faith` still shows their ordinary member view.

## Use a sacred book

Open **Scripture → Get sacred item**. Right-click the book to open its faith, or use it on a player to inspect them. The default collection cooldown is one minute.

## Find your way back

The blue **Back** button returns to the previous screen. At the first screen it becomes **Close**, with a red barrier icon. **Esc** does the same thing, so you can keep pressing it to leave the menus.

Closing a dialog does not necessarily stop an activity already running in the world. Follow the activity's controls; see [ceremonies](ceremonies.md).

Need to [leave your faith](join-faith.md#leave-or-switch-faith), [divorce](divorce.md), or [end an adoption](end-adoption.md)? Each has its own guide and confirmation.
