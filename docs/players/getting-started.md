# Getting started

Run `/faith` to find a community or open the faith you belong to. New players begin **Agnostic**, the neutral affiliation; you can stay there while exploring.

## Join a faith

1. Open `/faith` and select a faith. Already a member elsewhere? Use `/faith browse` to see the list.
2. Preview its **Teachings**, [Food](food.md), **Scripture** and [Ceremonies](ceremonies.md).
3. Choose **Join faith** for an open faith, or **Apply to join** and wait for its clergy. **Invitation only** means its clergy must invite you first.

Faiths are customizable communities. Their names do not guarantee particular food rules, ceremonies or teachings; read the rules shown on your server.

Want to found your own? Choose **Create faith** and follow [Create a faith](../leaders/create-faith.md).

## Find your way around

After joining, `/faith` opens your everyday practice menu. Use **Prayer**, **Food**, **People**, **Family**, **Scripture**, **Ceremonies** or **Your life** for the activity you need. `/faith settings` opens management tools for founders and clergy.

For an item shortcut, open **Scripture → Get sacred item**, then right-click that item to reopen your faith. The default wait between grants is 60 seconds. `/faith guide` reopens the optional **First steps** guide.

## Change faith

Open **Your life → Leave faith** and review the confirmation. You return to Agnostic and keep your family and history. Then browse and join another faith.

??? tip "If joining or leaving is unavailable"

    An application needs clergy approval. An invitation-only faith needs an invitation. If you already belong to another faith, leave it first. Founders with other members must choose a successor in **/faith settings → People** before leaving. If you cannot use `/faith`, ask the server owner to check your access.

Continue with [Prayer](prayer.md), [your family tree](family-tree.md) or [Your life](your-life.md).
