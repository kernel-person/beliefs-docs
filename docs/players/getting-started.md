# Start here

Run `/faith` to open your faith and family menu. You start in Agnostic and can stay there while exploring.

## Pick your first activity

- [Join a faith](join-faith.md), or [create your own](../leaders/create-faith.md).
- [Pray](prayer.md) and [join a ceremony](ceremonies.md).
- [Check what you can eat](food.md) or [see upcoming holy days](holy-days.md).
- [Marry](marriage.md), [adopt](adoption.md), or [open your family tree](family-tree.md).

Use `/faith guide` for **First steps**. Founders [edit their faith](../leaders/modify-faith.md) through `/faith settings`; `/faith` shows the member view.

## Use a sacred book

Open **Scripture → Get sacred item**. Right-click the book to open its faith, or use it on a player to inspect them. The default collection cooldown is one minute.

## Find your way back

Blue **Back** returns to the previous screen; red **Close** exits at the first screen. **Esc** does the same, so keep pressing it to leave the menus.

Closing a menu does not stop a ceremony already running. See its [cancellation controls](ceremonies.md).

To [leave a faith](join-faith.md#leave-or-switch-faith), [divorce](divorce.md), or [end an adoption](end-adoption.md), follow the separate guide and confirmation.
