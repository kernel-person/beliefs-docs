# How to divorce

Divorce removes **you** from your marriage group. You do not need a priest or permission from the other partners.

1. Run `/faith relationships` to open **Your family**.
2. Choose **Divorce**.
3. On **Leave marriage group?**, choose **Divorce** again to confirm. Choose **Stay married** to cancel.

<!-- Screenshot: Leave marriage group? confirmation with Divorce and Stay married visible. -->

You are no longer married to anyone in that group. If at least two partners remain, they stay married to one another. If only one remains, the group ends.

Your parent and child connections stay in place. Divorce also leaves your faith membership unchanged.

??? tip "I cannot see Divorce"

    The button appears when you have a completed marriage. A proposal waiting for consent is still a request: open `/faith pending` and choose **Cancel request** or **Decline** instead.

You can marry again through a new [proposal](marriage.md). To end a parent–child connection, use [End an adoption](end-adoption.md).
