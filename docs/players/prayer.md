# How to pray

<span id="pray-in-the-world"></span>

1. Open `/faith` → **Prayer**. Check the requirements and any red cooldown message.
2. Select **Begin prayer** to return to the world. Follow the action bar.
3. Hold sneak and stay still while the bar fills. The default hold is 3 seconds; moving or releasing sneak resets it.
4. Follow any remaining steps and stay nearby until the completion message.

<!-- Screenshot: the in-world prayer action bar while a player holds sneak. -->

If you already joined a ceremony, hold sneak when its prayer step begins. No second button is needed. Ordinary crouching outside an active prayer does nothing.

Shortcuts: **Prayer** in `/faith guide` starts it directly, as does `/faith pray`.

## If you cannot begin

- **On cooldown:** wait for the displayed time. Reopening the page checks the current wait.
- **Needs a priest or temple:** meet the requirements shown for this faith's prayer.
- **No prayer ceremony:** the founder must add one. Agnostic has no prayer of its own.
- **Another step comes first:** open **Ceremonies**, choose the ceremony and follow its steps.

A full bar finishes one prayer action. The ceremony may still be waiting for another participant or its minimum time. Check `/faith` or your sacred book for the next instruction.

<span id="optional-directional-blessing"></span>

??? info "Facing a holy place"

    If your faith offers this bonus, open **Ceremonies → Holy place**, hold a compass and choose **Bind compass**.

    Pray facing the place in the same world. The **Blessing** bar tracks the extra hold. A missing destination or blessing cooldown does not block ordinary prayer.

    Default bonus: a 10-second hold, Speed I for 30 seconds, then a 5-minute bonus cooldown. Your server can change these settings.

To stop, use **Ceremonies → Nearby / active → Stop worship** or `/faith cancel`. This cancels the ceremony for everyone. Closing the book does not stop it.
