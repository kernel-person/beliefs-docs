# Your life

Open **/faith → Your life** to see your current life score, standing and scored deeds. If you are Agnostic, `/faith view` opens your affiliation's overview with the same **Your life** link.

## Read your record

**Life score** totals this life's deeds for your current faith. **Standing** is your accumulated standing with that faith. The deed list groups scored actions and shows their count and most recent occurrence; it can also retain deeds from a faith you left.

A deed uses the teaching in force when it happened. Later changes to teachings do not rewrite its saved points. Read **Teachings** to understand future actions, and [Food](food.md) for dietary rules.

After death, choose **Last life** or run `/faith report`. Open a deed to read its explanation, point calculation and faith at the time. A fresh life begins while the completed report remains available.

??? info "Judgment and respawning"

    With default server thresholds, a score of at least +20 is **Blessed**, at most −20 is **Penance**, and scores between are **Neutral**. Blessed grants Luck for 120 seconds; Penance grants Weakness for 60 seconds.

    A faith that enables the Nether afterlife can give **Nether** judgment at −100 or below with at least five serious negative occurrences. This also applies Weakness. Nether respawn requires a safe destination configured by the server owner; otherwise normal respawn is used. These [server settings](../reference/configuration.md) can change.

## Remember a completed life

Open **Your life → Memorials**, select a completed life and optionally write a **Public inscription**. Choose **Begin remembrance**, then stay within **8 blocks for 20 seconds**. Others may join voluntarily. Place the resulting memorial head and right-click it to read.

??? tip "A report or memorial is missing"

    Your first completed life appears after death. If a completed remembrance is awaiting delivery, use **Collect head** with that life selected. Each memorial head can be collected once.

Leaving a faith preserves your history and [family](family-tree.md). See [Getting started](getting-started.md#change-faith) for the switching steps.
