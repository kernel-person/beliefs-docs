# How to join or leave a faith

Run `/faith browse` to see the communities on your server. You can follow one faith at a time; **Agnostic** is the neutral starting affiliation.

## Join a faith

1. Select a faith from the list.
2. Read its **Teachings**, **Food**, **Scripture**, or **Ceremonies** to see what it asks of followers.
3. Choose **Join faith** if membership is open. For an application faith, choose **Apply to join** and wait for clergy approval.

For an invitation-only faith, ask its clergy to invite you, then return to its page and choose **Join faith**. Applying does not change your membership until the application is approved.

Once you join, `/faith` opens your faith's everyday menu. Its name or symbol does not guarantee particular rules: every community is configurable.

## Leave or switch faith

1. Open `/faith` → **Your life**.
2. Choose **Leave faith** and review the confirmation.
3. Confirm to return to Agnostic. To join another community, open `/faith browse` again.

Your family, life history and recorded standing are retained.

??? tip "The button is unavailable"

    If you already belong to another faith, leave it before joining or creating a new one. Founders with followers must first choose a successor in **/faith settings → People → member → Transfer**. Then they can leave normally.

    An invitation-only community needs an invitation; an application needs approval. If you cannot use the menus at all, ask the server owner to check your permissions.

Prefer to start your own? Follow [How to create a faith](../leaders/create-faith.md).
